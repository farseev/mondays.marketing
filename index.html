The problem

Marketers are drowning in data and quietly short on confidence

The tools have never been more powerful, and many marketers have never felt less sure of themselves. Perfectionism, the sense of being undervalued and a quiet fear that AI will replace you all surface again and again when marketers talk about their work. Opinions are loud; evidence is scarce.

What is missing is not more information. It is one trusted signal at a time, and peers who can help you decide what it means. Confidence comes from collaboration and evidence, not from faking it till you make it.

About

Why Mondays

Monday is when plans get made. We wanted marketers to start the week with one thing they could trust and use, not a feed of hot takes. So SOMIN started a short weekly show: one real signal, decoded and made actionable, every Monday.

The show turned into a community because the questions people asked after each episode were too personal for a comment box. Will AI replace me? Am I measuring the right thing? Why did we lose that sale? Those get answered best in a room of peers, so we started meeting at Block71 Singapore.

The idea has not changed. Confidence through collaboration and evidence. Know it before you show it.

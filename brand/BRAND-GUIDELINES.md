# Marketing Mondays brand guidelines (v1.0, October 2026)

> Marketing Mondays is a SOMIN property and follows the SOMIN 2026 brand guideline: teal #2DBF9C, teal-black #16201F, Montserrat, sentence-case Brands voice, teal checkmark bullets, 3-up stat grids.

**Positioning:** The Singapore marketing community that replaces opinions with one evidenced signal a week, and fear with peers.
**Tagline:** One real signal, decoded every Monday
**Domain / contact:** mondays.marketing · ask@mondays.marketing
**Technology partner:** SOMIN (somin.ai). Show as "Technology partner SOMIN" with the two-tone SOMIN wordmark (SO teal #2DBF9C, MIN dark/white), never merged into our logo.

## Logo
The mark is a single teal pulse rising out of a flat line: one clear signal lifted out of the noise, every Monday.
Files: `brand/logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `brand/logo-dark.svg` (dark backgrounds), `brand/mark.svg` (icon), `favicon.svg`.
Clear space = height of the mark. Minimum 24 px tall. No recolouring, stretching, outlines or effects.

## Typography
- Headlines: Montserrat 800, tracking -0.02em, sentence case
- Body: Montserrat 400/500, 17 px / 1.65
- Labels: Montserrat 600, 12–13 px, uppercase, 0.2em tracking, accent colour
- Corner radius: 14px

## Personality
- Candid
- Evidence-led
- Generous
- Grounded

## Voice
Do:
- Lead with the signal and the evidence behind it
- Speak peer to peer, never down
- End with one clear action

Don't:
- Hype, buzzwords or trend lists
- Invented numbers or quotes
- Making people feel behind

## Imagery
Editorial, artistic illustration in teal #2DBF9C on deep teal-black #16201F with white accents; single bright signals against noise, small groups of people, Singapore cues. No stock handshakes. All imagery generated with SOMIN image generation; no text in images, no stock, no identifiable real people.

## Calls to action
Every primary CTA ("Request an invite") opens an email to ask@mondays.marketing.
